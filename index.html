Research
ME-Prune: Robust Neural Network Compression via Generalization Metrics-informed Structured Pruning
E. Ye, A. Zhao, Y. Yang, Y. Zhou, K. Ramchandran, M. Mahoney.

ME-Prune compresses a neural network from an out-of-distribution (OOD) robustness perspective, focusing on mitigating classification errors that result from common everyday corruptions modeled by data augmentation schemes. Using random matrix theory metrics from statistical physics, we perform network structure search and channel pruning to reach state-of-the-art performance.

NewsPod: Automatic and Interactive News Podcasts
P. Laban, E. Ye, J. Canny, M. Hearst.
ACM Conference on Intelligent User Interfaces, 2022. Accepted.

An interactive and automated news podcast, NewsPods presents top stories in the format of simulated conversations. More than 80% of participants across multiple iterations of our user study reported they would use NewsPod to stay informed, and 85% actively interacted with the podcast. Participant-prompted questions reveal future research directions in natural language processing methods and question-answering models.
